Levels (datums)

Every wall is hosted on a level. Elevation is the top of subfloor for that level. Changing an elevation moves every wall on that level.

Header tables

Header sizes come from the plans or general notes. When one isn't called out, students use the Header table button in the Frame Wall form. Wall Framer ships with the SFPA Southern Pine tables; import your own to replace them.
